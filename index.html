Crafting the Creative Spark

By Benson Yeung

About

Competitive programming is often seen as a collection of algorithms, data structures, and contest results. Yet behind every memorable problem lies a story – a strange observation, an unexpected configuration, a late-night discussion, or a moment where a simple idea suddenly clicks into place.

Curated within these pages are 16 problems selected from over 70 tasks I have authored for various contests. More than a technical manual, this is a reflection on the 'spirit of problem solving' – a reminder that the heart of the craft remains the joy of discovery.
